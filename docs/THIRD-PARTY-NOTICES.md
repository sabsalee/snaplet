# Third-party components

- **.NET 10 / Windows Desktop runtime** — Microsoft and .NET contributors. Self-contained distribution includes the runtime's license and third-party notice files where supplied by the runtime pack. Project: https://github.com/dotnet/runtime and https://github.com/dotnet/wpf
- **Tesseract .NET 5.2.0** — Charles Weld and contributors; Apache-2.0. https://github.com/charlesw/tesseract/tree/5.2.0
- **Tesseract OCR and Leptonica native binaries** — native redistributables included by the Tesseract NuGet package. Tesseract: Apache-2.0, https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tesseract ; Leptonica: BSD-2-Clause, https://github.com/DanBloomberg/leptonica . See the `licenses` directory for bundled text and corresponding wrapper redistribution notices.
- **tessdata_fast English/Korean models** — Tesseract contributors; Apache-2.0. Exact revision, source URLs and SHA-256 digests: `tessdata/provenance.json`. License text: `tessdata/LICENSE`.

- **Velopack 1.2.161** — Velopack Ltd., Caelan Sayler and contributors; MIT. https://github.com/velopack/velopack . See `licenses/Velopack-1.2.161-LICENSE.txt`.

- **ZXing.Net 0.16.9** — Michael Jahn and contributors (port of ZXing); Apache-2.0. Used locally to decode QR and other 2D codes. https://github.com/micjahn/ZXing.Net . See `licenses/ZXing.Net-0.16.9-LICENSE.txt`.
